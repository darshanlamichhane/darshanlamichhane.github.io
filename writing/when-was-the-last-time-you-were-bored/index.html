When was the last time you were bored?

By Darshan Lamichhane

We, as a civilization, have always wanted things faster.

Faster answers.
Quicker entertainment.
Less waiting.

With technology & AI, we are better at that than ever.

But I have been thinking about something.

When was the last time you actually spent time with a stupid idea?

Actually, when was the last time you were bored?

AI & tech aren’t the problem. It’s just how well things are built around human psychology, around convenient answers.

Go inward & find your muse then,
AI is taking our job anyway, right?

Most of us are consumers in that system.

Creators seem to be a bit different.

Christopher Nolan famously doesn’t carry a smartphone.

All this made me think of a weird question:

If Newton were born today, had a phone in his pocket, phone constantly buzzing, & every entertainment, & an AI he could ask anything, would he still have spent enough time with his thoughts to do what he did?

So then:

What’s stopping me from creating?
What’s stopping you from creating?

Maybe procrastination, perfectionism & fear?

We should let ourselves have stupid, bad & naive thoughts. Definitely not asking AI to make it better.
